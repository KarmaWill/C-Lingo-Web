July 8, 2026 · Strategic Partnership

NSK Signs MOU with HEC Pakistan

On July 8, NSK signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) on strategic cooperation with the Higher Education Commission of Pakistan in Beijing. The MOU will focus on cooperation in AI education, international Chinese language education, smart education platform development, and interdisciplinary talent cultivation. The signing marks a new stage in NSK’s overseas business, upgrading from language learning product output to digital full-stack solution deployment.

Witnessed by Prof. Xu Baofeng, Dean of the College of Sinology and Chinese Studies (Belt and Road Research Institute) of Beijing Language and Culture University (BLCU) and Director of the World Sinology Center; Mr. Han Zhihua, Vice President of NSK; and Prof. Dr. Niaz Ahmad Akhtar (SI), Chairman of the Higher Education Commission of Pakistan, the MOU was signed on behalf of both parties.

With its core strengths in full-stack AI technology, standardized Chinese education systems, all-scenario product matrix, and scalable implementation experience, NSK can provide Pakistan with systematic and practical value support across five areas: education infrastructure, Chinese teaching system development, bilingual talent cultivation, industrial services, and people-to-people exchange.

Three Key Areas of Cooperation

The MOU will focus on three key areas:

  • Standardized international Chinese language education centers — supporting the construction of centers with an overall plan covering about 10 institutions.
  • National Smart Education Public Service Platform — jointly developing the platform to promote education resource connectivity, high-quality course co-development, and standardized teaching system implementation.
  • China-Pakistan cultural and educational exchange brand events — jointly building events including the International Chinese Language and Vocational Skills Competition and the National Sinology Competition of Pakistan.

About the Higher Education Commission of Pakistan

The Higher Education Commission of Pakistan is the statutory and highly autonomous supreme authority of higher education at the federal level of Pakistan, with its Chairman enjoying federal ministerial status. HEC carries out core functions including higher education policy and standard formulation, institutional accreditation and quality assurance, federal funding allocation and financial management, research support, and industry-academia-research transformation. It oversees 265 higher education institutions nationwide. The MOU was signed on behalf of the Commission by its Chairman, Prof. Dr. Niaz Ahmad Akhtar (SI).

At present, Pakistan is accelerating cooperation with China in AI, vocational education, digital transformation, and industrial skills development. The upgrading of the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor is also driving growing demand for talents with both Chinese language proficiency and professional skills. More than 30 universities in Pakistan have opened Chinese language courses, with nearly 10,000 students learning Chinese each year. According to media reports, more than 38,000 Pakistani students are currently studying at universities in China. As Chinese learning needs expand from university classrooms to study abroad, employment, and industrial service scenarios, Pakistan’s demand for standardized Chinese teaching systems, digital education platforms, and sustainable exchange mechanisms is becoming increasingly clear.

Looking ahead, NSK will continue to build on its AI education technology and the product ecosystem of its AI language learning brand C-Lingo, working with more universities, educational institutions, and partners to explore sustainable pathways for AI education technology to serve international Chinese language education, educational digitalization, and interdisciplinary talent cultivation.

May 23, 2026 · Press Release

NSK Debuts C-Lingo: A New Era of AI-Powered Chinese Language Learning

As globalization deepens, the global community of Chinese language learners is expanding rapidly, driving an urgent demand for high-quality digital educational experiences. On May 23, 2026, Hong Kong-based innovation firm Hong Kong NSK IT Limited (NSK) officially launched the global digital-intelligent Chinese learning solution for its flagship brand, C-Lingo. As a pioneer in the field of global autonomous Chinese learning, C-Lingo is committed to breaking geographical boundaries through its proprietary "Connected Learning" architecture, providing learners worldwide with high-quality, high-efficiency digital Chinese education.

Optimized for AI: Elevating Global Chinese Education

As NSK's core brand for the global market, C-Lingo focuses on the field of digital-intelligent Chinese language learning. Currently, Chinese language education is experiencing rapid growth globally, with 90 countries having incorporated Chinese into their national curricula or syllabi, and the global learner population exceeding 210 million. Against this backdrop, C-Lingo fuses authoritative pedagogical content with AI technology to provide global learners with more scientific and efficient digital learning tools, making autonomous Chinese learning simpler and more systematic.

In terms of content, the brand aligns deeply with the HSK3.0 (Chinese Proficiency Test) standards and integrates the Happy Chinese textbook—a key curriculum planned by the Center for Language Education and Cooperation (CLEC) under China's Ministry of Education, building a scientific and standardized knowledge system for global learners. With AI assistance, static textbook content is transformed into intelligent interactive learning experiences, allowing overseas learners to enjoy high-quality Chinese language services anytime and anywhere via smart devices. This model, which combines authoritative teaching materials with AI-driven tools, serves as the core foundation for C-Lingo's continued support for learners worldwide.

Connected Learning: A Closed-Loop for Language Acquisition

To translate AI technology into measurable learning outcomes, C-Lingo has pioneered the "Connected Learning" methodology. Anchored by the proprietary AIDEO pedagogical system and a robust Chinese language knowledge graph, this framework precisely deconstructs authoritative textbooks and standardized curricula. By synergizing AI-powered personalized learning engines with gamified incentives, it transforms static content into sustainable, digital progress paths, truly achieving the shift from "passive symbols" to "active expression" for learners.

This core methodology is perfectly realized through C-Lingo's smart hardware matrix, which features high-performance offline modes, ensuring a premium experience for learners globally regardless of their environment:

  • AI-Powered Learning Tablet: Serving as the core for deep home-based learning, the tablet deeply integrates a full-process closed-loop cycle of "Teaching, Learning, Practice, Assessment, Evaluation, and Exploration". Through AI-driven dynamic path matching, it makes learning more scientific and highly efficient.
  • AI Chinese Reading Pen: Supporting full-scenario offline decoding, this tool serves as an extension for ubiquitous learning, helping students integrate Chinese language applications into their daily lives and effectively breaking the constraints of the classroom.

Beyond the Classroom: Rethinking International Chinese Education

Over the past several years, I have worked deeply in international Chinese education—supporting classroom instruction, learner-centered programs, and observing how Chinese is taught across diverse global settings. From complete beginners to advanced HSK learners, these experiences taught me that international Chinese education is far more than language instruction. It is an interdisciplinary field that combines pedagogy, communication, cultural understanding, and educational technology.

What truly inspired me was realizing that language learning is ultimately about human connection. Many learners I worked with were not simply trying to memorize vocabulary or pass exams. They wanted to communicate, understand Chinese culture, and build genuine cross-cultural relationships. At the same time, I often saw how traditional methods failed to bridge the gap between classroom knowledge and real-world communication.

When Language Barriers Are Cultural Barriers

One moment stayed with me. I worked with a student who excelled in written exercises but became unsure during a classroom discussion about family relationships—a topic that seems simple. Later I realized the difficulty was not linguistic, but cultural. In her native culture, certain family expressions carried very different emotional meanings than in Chinese. She was not struggling with words; she was trying to understand the context behind them.

Language barriers are often cultural barriers in disguise. True learning happens when learners understand not just what to say, but why people say it that way. Seeing learners persevere through these challenges strengthened my belief that accessible, structured tools matter—and why C-Lingo's vision resonated with me.

Why C-Lingo

What attracted me most was C-Lingo's vision of combining AI and educational innovation to make Chinese learning more connected, personalized, and culturally meaningful—to build a system that is more accessible, structured, and engaging. This is the direction international Chinese education needs to move toward.

Moving from teaching into business development has broadened my perspective. C-Lingo is not just a product. It connects learners, structured Chinese knowledge, AI intelligence, smart devices, and real-world scenarios into a continuous, adaptive learning system—redefining how international Chinese learning happens.
